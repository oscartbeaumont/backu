Backu
Your photos, your bucket

Your photos.
Your bucket.

Back up, browse and search your whole photo library on storage you already own. No account, no subscription, no server in the middle.

What it does

  • Backs up originals, videos and screenshots to any S3-compatible bucket.
  • Photos-style library, full-screen viewer, tags and details.
  • On-device face and pet grouping you can name.
  • Instant search by person, place, camera, tag or year.
  • Automatic background backup, Wi-Fi only if you prefer.
  • Multiple servers and readable JSON sidecars beside every photo.
