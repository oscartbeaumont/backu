Privacy Policy

Backu for iPhone, iPad and Mac · Effective 17 November 2026

The short version: Backu has no account, no analytics and no developer server. Your photos go only to the storage bucket you configure. Face recognition runs on your device and its feature vectors never leave it.

Who operates Backu

Backu is an open-source app made by Oscar Beaumont. Questions can be raised at github.com/oscartbeaumont/backu/issues.

What the app accesses

  • Photo library. With your permission, Backu reads your photos and videos to display them, build an on-device index, analyse faces, and back them up. You can grant limited access or revoke it at any time in System Settings.
  • Photo metadata. Capture date, camera, lens, exposure, dimensions and location embedded in your photos are read and stored with the photo's index entry and sidecar.
  • Storage credentials. The access key ID and secret you enter for an S3-compatible service are stored in the system Keychain on your device and are used only to sign requests to the endpoint you entered.

Where your data goes

  • Photo files, thumbnails, tags, people names, face boxes and search metadata are uploaded only to the bucket you configure. The developer does not operate a server and does not receive a copy.
  • Your storage provider processes data under its own privacy policy. Backu has no relationship with that provider on your behalf and no ability to read your bucket.
  • Face feature vectors are stored only on your device in a file excluded from iCloud device backups. They are never uploaded.
  • Backu contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting SDK.

Retention and deletion

  • You control the bucket: delete objects there at any time.
  • Removing the app deletes its local catalog, search index, previews and face vectors from the device. Photos already uploaded remain in your bucket until you delete them.
  • “Reset library index” in Settings removes the local index, face groups and cached previews without touching the bucket.

Donations

The in-app Support card opens GitHub Sponsors in your browser. GitHub processes that visit under its own privacy policy; Backu receives only the information GitHub Sponsors makes available to a sponsor recipient, and never receives payment details.

Children

Backu is a general-purpose photo tool, not directed at children under 13, and has no account or social features.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date. The app's source code is public, so the data behaviour can be audited at github.com/oscartbeaumont/backu.
