# Test fixtures

These images exist only to exercise the app in the simulator and the test suite.
They are not part of the app and are regenerated by
`swift Tools/Fixtures/prepare-fixtures.swift`, which writes resized JPEGs with
synthetic EXIF (camera, lens, GPS) into `Tests/Fixtures/library/`.
